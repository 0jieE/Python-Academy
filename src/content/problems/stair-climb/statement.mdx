The gym has a staircase with `N` steps. A student climbs it taking **1, 2 or 3
steps** at a time and always ends exactly on the top step.

Two climbs are different if the sequence of step sizes is different — so
`1 + 2` and `2 + 1` count as two ways. How many different ways are there?

The number grows very fast, so print it modulo `1 000 000 007`.
